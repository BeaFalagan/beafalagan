## 📂 Featured projects

### ⭐ [centinelAI](https://centinelai.io) · AI observability for DevOps teams

*"Your SRE that never sleeps"*: from 1,000 alerts a day to the 3 interruptions that matter. A product I build end to end, now in open beta.

- **AI pipeline:** rule-based deduplication, 0–100 scoring and correlation with Claude Haiku, plus automatic postmortems with Claude Sonnet. Only critical alerts reach Slack, with probable cause, impact and recommended actions.
- **Connectors:** an open-source, read-only Kubernetes agent in Python, plus webhooks for Prometheus/Alertmanager, Grafana and GitLab CI/CD.
- **Serverless architecture:** Next.js 14 on Vercel, Supabase (Postgres + pgvector + Auth), durable jobs with Inngest, payments with Stripe and email with Resend.
- **End-to-end product:** public sign-up, plans and billing, [documentation](https://docs.centinelai.io) and CI with automated tests.

[Website](https://centinelai.io) · [Docs](https://docs.centinelai.io) · [GitHub](https://github.com/centinel-AI)
